Research

  • Efficient LLM inference. Speculative decoding (draft–verifier pairs, acceptance-rate vs. speedup trade-offs, adaptive draft length), INT8/FP16 post-training quantization, structured attention-head pruning, and KV-cache strategies for serving models under tight memory budgets.
  • ML for network security. Intrusion detection models that transfer across network domains through unified flow representations, and the robustness of those detectors against adversarial evasion attacks and the defenses that counter them.
  • Secure and trustworthy inference systems. Where the two threads meet: how the optimizations that make inference fast change what a system reveals and how it can be attacked.

Publications

ICDAM2026

A Cross-Domain Intrusion Detection System Using Unified Network Flow Representations and Deep Models

Suraj Prasad Kalauni, et al.

International Conference on Data Analytics & Management (ICDAM), 2026

Presented · ICDAM 2026

Intrusion detection models trained on one network dataset often degrade sharply on another, because each dataset encodes traffic with its own feature set. This work maps heterogeneous datasets into a unified network-flow representation and evaluates deep detection models in a cross-domain setting.

ICDAM2026

Adversarial Evasion Attacks and Defense Strategies for ML-Based Network Intrusion Detection on CICIDS2017

Suraj Prasad Kalauni, et al.

International Conference on Data Analytics & Management (ICDAM), 2026

Presented · ICDAM 2026

ML-based intrusion detectors reach high accuracy on benchmark traffic, but an attacker can perturb flow features to slip past them. This paper studies adversarial evasion attacks against detectors trained on CICIDS2017 and evaluates defense strategies against them.

AIP2025

Retinal Fundus Image and Deep Learning: A Novel Approach for Cardiovascular Risk Prediction

Suraj Prasad Kalauni, et al.

AIP Conference Proceedings, 2025

DOI

The retina offers a non-invasive view of the body's microvasculature. This work introduces a deep learning approach that predicts cardiovascular risk directly from retinal fundus images, reaching 79.41% accuracy in classifying cardiovascular risk.

2025

From-scratch PyTorch implementation of draft–verify decoding (GPT-2 draft, GPT-2 XL target), reproducing the acceptance/rejection scheme of Leviathan et al. Five controlled experiments across model pairs (incl. Qwen and Llama), draft length K, temperature, adaptive K, and KV-cache strategies.
